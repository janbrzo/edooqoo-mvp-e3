## TECHNICAL FEATURES

### What is DSLM (Dynamic Student Learning Model)?

DSLM is Edooqoo's proprietary 4-layer system: Layer A (Event Log) captures every student interaction with timestamps and skill tags. Layer B (Metrics) aggregates events into nano-skill scores using exponential decay weighting (recent performance weighted more). Layer C (Profile) synthesizes metrics into a student learning profile with strengths, weaknesses, and trends. Layer D (Decision Engine) drives AI recommendations for future worksheets based on skill gaps.

### What is nano-skill tracking?

Nano-skills are the smallest measurable units of language competence in Edooqoo. Example: "B1.grammar.present_perfect.negative" tracks a specific grammar point at a specific CEFR level. This granularity enables precise progress tracking — teachers see exactly which skills are improving, stable, or declining. Skills are organized in categories: grammar, vocabulary, speaking, listening, reading, writing.

### How does SM-2 spaced repetition work in Edooqoo?

Edooqoo's flashcards use the SM-2 algorithm: students rate recall quality (0-5 scale), the algorithm calculates optimal review intervals based on easiness factor and repetition count. Cards rated poorly appear sooner; well-known cards appear less frequently. This produces 90%+ retention rates compared to ~50% with traditional study methods. Supports bidirectional study (front→back and back→front).

### What exercise types does Edooqoo offer?

29 exercise types in 3 categories. Basic (20): Reading Comprehension, Fill in the Blanks, Multiple Choice, True/False, Matching, Dialogue Practice, Answer Questions, Discussion Questions, Error Correction, Odd One Out, Matching Halves, Word Order, Gap Text, Negative Prefixes, Categorization, Complete Word, Paraphrasing, Sentence Transformation, Synonyms Matching, Antonyms Matching. Audio (5): Listening Comprehension, Fill in the Blanks (Audio), Multiple Choice (Audio), True/False (Audio), Answer Questions (Audio). Picture (4): Describe Picture, Multiple Choice (Picture), True/False (Picture), Answer Questions (Picture).

### How does Edooqoo's AI personalize worksheets?

Three layers of personalization: 1) CEFR level — AI adjusts vocabulary, grammar, text length, and difficulty. 2) Student Knowledge — teachers add notes about interests, weaknesses, goals; AI uses these in generation. 3) DSLM skill data — AI identifies skill gaps and generates exercises targeting weak areas. Result: every worksheet is unique and relevant to the individual student.

### How does the Student Hub work?

Students visit https://edooqoo.com/my, enter their email, and select their teacher. The portal shows: quick stats (flashcards reviewed, homework completed, lessons scheduled), shared worksheets, homework assignments with deadlines, flashcard sets for spaced repetition study, lesson booking (browse and book available slots), and Google Calendar sync. No teacher account needed for students.

A 49-question AI-powered assessment: 10 grammar questions, 10 vocabulary questions, 10 reading comprehension questions, 10 listening exercises (AI-generated audio), 9 speaking prompts (record audio responses). AI analyzes results and creates a Learning Profile with: estimated CEFR level, skill scores per category, strengths/weaknesses, Learning Path recommendation (Comfort, Guided, Accelerated, or Target), and 15 behavioral signals.

Teachers connect Google Calendar in Calendar Settings. All lesson bookings, cancellations, and reschedules sync automatically. Optional: Google Meet link auto-generation for each booking. Students can also connect their Google Calendar via Student Hub. Customizable: event colors by status, reminders, sync preferences.

## LESSON CALENDAR & BOOKING

### How does lesson booking work in Edooqoo?

Teachers set their availability in the Lesson Calendar, then share a public booking page link with students. Students enter their email, browse available time slots by week, and book lessons — including weekly recurring bookings. The teacher can configure booking modes (auto-confirm or require confirmation), buffer times between lessons, slot limits per student per week, and cancellation policies. All bookings sync to Google Calendar automatically.

### Does Edooqoo integrate with Google Calendar?

Yes. Teachers connect their Google Calendar via OAuth. All lesson bookings, confirmations, cancellations, and reschedules sync automatically. Google Meet links are auto-generated per student. Students can also connect their own Google Calendar via the Student Hub — with customizable event colors per status (booked, pending, completed, no-show) and sync toggles.

### What is Live Session mode?

Live Session mode lets teachers conduct real-time lessons using any worksheet. The teacher opens a worksheet, activates Live Session, and shares the permanent link with students. Students access the worksheet in their browser and complete exercises. The teacher sees all answers in real-time as students type, can add exercises on-the-fly (up to 12 per session), and rate nano-skill mastery per exercise. AI evaluation assists with scoring open-ended exercises.

### Can students book lessons independently?

Yes. Teachers share a public booking page URL. Students enter their email, browse available slots, and book lessons without teacher intervention. Supports one-time and weekly recurring bookings. Students can also book, reschedule, and cancel via the Student Hub portal.

### How does reschedule work in Edooqoo?

Students request a reschedule via the Student Hub. The system creates a new pending slot and notifies the teacher. The teacher confirms or rejects. On confirm: old slot is freed, new slot is confirmed, both Google Calendars are updated. On reject: the pending slot is removed from both calendars. Confirmation emails include the meeting link.

### What is the Drawing Canvas?

The Draw on Worksheet feature provides an in-browser drawing overlay for annotating worksheets during online lessons. Teachers can highlight text, circle answers, draw diagrams, and add visual notes directly on the worksheet. No external whiteboard tool needed.
