AI Lesson Planning for English Teachers

Edooqoo uses AI to streamline the entire lesson planning workflow for English teachers. From generating worksheets to suggesting topics based on student progress data, the platform handles material creation so teachers can focus on teaching. Teachers report saving 3-5 hours per week on average.

Traditional lesson planning for English teachers involves finding appropriate texts, creating exercises, preparing answer keys, and adapting materials to different student levels — a process that takes 30-60 minutes per lesson. With Edooqoo, the entire process takes under 2 minutes.

But Edooqoo goes beyond worksheet generation. It's a complete lesson planning ecosystem: AI suggests topics based on skill gaps, the calendar system schedules lessons, homework extends learning between sessions, flashcards reinforce vocabulary, and progress tracking shows what worked and what needs more practice.

AI Lesson Planning Workflow

  1. Review student progress — Check DSLM metrics to identify skill gaps and areas needing practice
  2. Use AI suggestions — Edooqoo suggests worksheet topics and exercises based on student data
  3. Generate worksheet — Create a personalized worksheet with targeted exercises in under 60 seconds
  4. Teach the lesson — Use Live Session mode for real-time interaction or share the worksheet
  5. Assign homework — Select exercises for additional practice with AI grading
  6. Create flashcards — Add new vocabulary to spaced repetition flashcard sets
  7. Track results — Review homework scores and update progress tracking
  8. Plan next lesson — AI uses updated data to suggest the next focus area

Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI suggest lesson topics?

Edooqoo's AI analyzes the student's DSLM (Dynamic Student Learning Model) data — skill gaps, declining trends, and unaddressed CEFR-tagged competencies — to suggest targeted worksheet topics and exercise types.

Can I plan recurring lessons?

Yes. The Lesson Calendar supports weekly recurring bookings with Google Calendar sync. You can plan a series of lessons and generate materials for each one progressively.

How does Edooqoo compare to generic AI tools like ChatGPT?

Unlike ChatGPT, Edooqoo provides structured exercises with answer keys, interactive student completion, automatic grading, progress tracking, homework management, flashcards, and lesson scheduling — all in one platform specifically designed for English teachers.

Can I plan lessons for multiple students?

Yes. Each student has their own profile, progress data, and lesson history. Generate unique worksheets for each student based on their individual needs and CEFR level.

Does lesson planning work for online teaching?

Absolutely. Edooqoo integrates with Google Meet for video calls, supports Live Session mode for real-time interaction, and all materials are accessible online. The Lesson Calendar handles timezone management for international students.
