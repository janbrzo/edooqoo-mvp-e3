Edooqoo — AI Worksheet Generator for English Teachers

What is Edooqoo?

Edooqoo is an AI-powered platform built specifically for English teachers. It generates personalized worksheets tailored to each student in under 2 minutes, with 29 exercise types covering all CEFR levels from A1 to C2. Unlike generic worksheet libraries, Edooqoo creates unique, custom content every time — based on your student's level, interests, learning goals, and actual skill data.

Edooqoo is a complete teaching ecosystem: assign homework with automatic AI grading, create smart flashcards with spaced repetition, schedule lessons with Google Calendar sync, run placement tests, and track student progress at the nano-skill level. Students get their own portal — the Student Hub — where they study independently.

Used by private English tutors, ESL/EFL instructors, Business English coaches, language school teachers, online English teachers, and corporate language trainers worldwide.

Who is Edooqoo For?

  • Private English Tutors — Generate personalized worksheets for each student. Track individual progress and assign homework between lessons.
  • Language School Teachers — Quickly create varied materials for different class levels. Save hours on preparation and grading.
  • Business English Coaches — Create industry-specific worksheets for corporate clients. Track professional development with detailed skill metrics.
  • Online ESL/EFL Teachers — Use Live Session mode for real-time online lessons. Manage bookings across time zones.
  • Exam Preparation Tutors — Select exercise types matching exam formats. Identify weak areas with progress tracking.
  • Corporate Language Trainers — Schedule sessions, generate company-specific content, provide progress reports.

Generate complete, personalized worksheets in under 2 minutes. Choose from 29 exercise types across 3 categories. The AI uses student context to create unique content every time.

Smart Flashcards

Create flashcard sets from worksheet vocabulary or manually. Students study using the SM-2 spaced repetition algorithm.

Lesson Calendar & Booking

Teachers set availability, students book via a public booking page. Google Calendar two-way sync with Google Meet auto-generation.

49-question AI-powered assessment covering grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, and speaking. AI creates a detailed Learning Profile.

  • 49 questions across 5 skill categories

Student Hub Portal

Dedicated portal for students. Access worksheets, flashcards, homework, and lesson booking without a teacher account.

  • Personal dashboard with quick stats

Student Progress Tracking (DSLM)

Dynamic Student Learning Model tracks skills at the nano-skill level with CEFR tags. Every interaction updates metrics automatically.

  • Category view: grammar, vocabulary, speaking, listening, reading, writing
  • AI suggestions for future worksheets

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be logged in to generate worksheets?
No, you can try the worksheet generator without logging in (demo mode). To save worksheets and access all features, create an account. You get 2 free tokens when you sign up.
What happens when I run out of tokens?
You can upgrade to a subscription plan for monthly worksheets or purchase additional tokens. The system first uses your monthly allowance, then tokens.
How does the upgrade/downgrade system work?
Upgrade anytime and pay only the prorated difference. Downgrades take effect at your next billing cycle. Unused monthly worksheets roll over as tokens.
What exercise types are available?
29 exercise types in 3 categories: 20 Basic, 5 Audio, 4 Picture. All available on all plans including Free.
How long does worksheet generation take?
Typically 30-60 seconds. The AI creates custom content based on level, topic, goals, and student context.
What is the Student Hub?
A dedicated portal for students. They enter their email, select their teacher, and access worksheets, flashcards, homework, and lesson booking — without needing a teacher account.
How does AI Evaluation work?
AI automatically evaluates open-ended answers and pre-fills mastery scores (0-100). Teachers can review, adjust, and confirm scores.
What is the Welcome Test?
A 49-question AI-powered assessment covering grammar, vocabulary, reading, listening, and speaking. Creates a detailed Learning Profile with CEFR level and Learning Path.
How does Lesson Booking work?
Teachers share a public booking page. Students book lessons including recurring weekly slots. Google Calendar sync and Google Meet auto-generation included.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, cancel anytime via Stripe Customer Portal. Your subscription remains active until the end of your billing period.
Is there a limit on students?
No limit on students. Student management is available for all registered users at no additional cost.
Can I use it for commercial purposes?
Yes, all plans support commercial use including private tutoring, language schools, and corporate training.
