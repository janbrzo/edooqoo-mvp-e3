2. Twee — Quick AI Text Generation for Teachers

Twee focuses on generating reading texts, dialogues, and basic exercises from topics. It's fast and simple but lacks student management, homework features, and audio/picture exercises. Best for teachers who just need quick text generation without a full teaching platform.

3. MagicSchool — General-Purpose AI for K-12 Teachers

MagicSchool offers 50+ AI tools for all subjects and grade levels. While versatile, it's not specialized for English language teaching — no CEFR support, no ESL-specific exercise types, no student progress tracking for language skills.

4. Diffit — Reading Level Differentiation

Diffit excels at adapting reading materials to different grade levels. Useful for content-based instruction but limited in exercise variety and completely lacks vocabulary, grammar, listening, and speaking exercise generation.

5. Monsha — AI Lesson Planner

Monsha generates lesson plans and assessment materials for various subjects. Good for curriculum planning but not deep enough for ESL-specific material creation. No audio exercises, no flashcards, no student hub.

  • Homework & grading — Can students complete exercises online with automatic AI evaluation?
  • Student management — Does it track student progress and skill mastery over time?
  • Audio & visual content — Can it generate listening exercises and picture-based activities?
