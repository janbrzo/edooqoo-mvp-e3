AI Worksheet Generator for English Teachers

Edooqoo is an AI-powered worksheet generator built specifically for English language teachers. It creates personalized, curriculum-aligned worksheets for ESL/EFL students in under 60 seconds. Unlike generic AI tools, Edooqoo understands CEFR levels (A1 through C2), offers 29 specialized exercise types, and adapts content to each student's proficiency level, interests, and learning goals.

Whether you teach private one-on-one lessons, manage a language school, coach Business English, or run online ESL classes, Edooqoo eliminates hours of manual material preparation. Teachers report saving 3-5 hours per week on average by switching from manual worksheet creation to AI-generated materials.

The platform goes beyond worksheet generation: it includes homework assignment with AI grading, smart flashcards with spaced repetition (SM-2 algorithm), a 49-question AI placement test, a Student Hub portal, lesson calendar with Google Calendar sync, and nano-skill student progress tracking.

How Edooqoo's AI Worksheet Generator Works

  1. Select your student — Choose the student from your roster. Edooqoo uses their learning profile, CEFR level, strengths, weaknesses, and interests to personalize content.
  2. Configure the worksheet — Set the topic (e.g., "travel vocabulary"), grammar focus (e.g., "past simple vs. present perfect"), CEFR level (A1-C2), and select from 29 exercise types.
  3. AI generates in seconds — The AI creates a complete, ready-to-use worksheet with exercises, answer keys, vocabulary lists, and audio/picture elements if selected.
  4. Share and track — Share the worksheet via a permanent link, assign as homework, use in a live session, or download as HTML/PDF.
